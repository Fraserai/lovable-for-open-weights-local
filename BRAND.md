# Brand

## Mission
**We're the shovel that lets anyone, and any business, build and own their own intelligence.**

We focus on one thing: building the best tool in this space, to open this world up to users, builders
and businesses. That's it.

## Lines
The lines we build the company, product, teams and culture around. Use them in marketing, copy and UI.

> **From Models to Moats.**
>
> **Build Intelligence. Own It.**
>
> **Train Models. Build Moats.**
>
> **Your Models. Your Intelligence.**

⚖️

## Using them
- Use the lines as written. Don't reword them or build new slogans from pieces of them.
- In the product they live in `web/src/lib/brand.ts`, so copy comes from one place.
- Who we're for: users, builders and businesses. Not only ML experts, and not one use case.
- "Own" is literal: the customer walks away with weights they control (download, their own repo,
  their own endpoint). Every feature should make that more true.
